OUR SERVICES

Cyber Law Assistance

Digital fraud, online harassment, unauthorised access, impersonation, social-media abuse and other technology-related incidents can create both immediate and long-term legal concerns. We provide legal guidance for individuals and businesses dealing with cyber-related issues.

CYBER LAW

Cyber Law & Digital Dispute Assistance

Cyber incidents often require prompt preservation of digital information. Screenshots, transaction records, emails, messages, URLs, account details and other electronic records may become important in understanding and documenting what occurred. We help clients identify the relevant facts and legal issues and understand the appropriate reporting or legal route.

Depending on the circumstances, assistance may include preparation of complaints or representations, legal notices, response to online misconduct, digital-payment fraud-related guidance, harassment matters and court-related legal assistance. We also advise clients on organising available electronic records for further legal action.

Cyber & Digital Matters

  • Online financial fraud and unauthorised digital transactions
  • Cyber harassment, threats, stalking and online abuse
  • Social-media impersonation and misuse of identity
  • Unauthorised access and account-related disputes
  • Defamatory or harmful online content and related legal concerns
  • Digital evidence organisation and documentation
  • Cyber-related legal notices, complaints and representations
  • Court proceedings and legal remedies arising from technology-related disputes

Act Quickly and Preserve Relevant Records

Digital evidence can be easily deleted, altered or lost. Early legal consultation can help a client understand what information should be preserved, how the incident should be documented and which legal or reporting options may be relevant to the circumstances.
