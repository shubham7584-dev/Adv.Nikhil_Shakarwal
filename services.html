WHAT WE DO

Legal Services

Adv. Nikhil Shakarwal provides legal assistance across a range of matters, with a focus on clear advice, careful preparation and client-focused support.

Court & Authority Matters

Legal assistance in miscellaneous work before courts, DDA, MCD, Income Tax Department, tribunals and other government or statutory authorities, including applications, representations, replies and procedural support.

View Service

Criminal Law

Legal assistance and representation in criminal matters including FIR-related issues, bail proceedings, criminal trials, defence strategy and appeals. Clients receive guidance on the legal process, documentation and representation at the appropriate stage.

Civil Law

Legal support in civil disputes involving recovery, contracts, injunctions, property-related disagreements and other civil proceedings. The service includes document review, legal advice, drafting and representation before the appropriate forum.

Family Law

Legal guidance in family and matrimonial matters including maintenance, child custody, domestic violence, family disputes and settlement-related issues. The focus is on protecting legal rights while handling sensitive matters with confidentiality.

Divorce Cases

Professional assistance in mutual and contested divorce matters, including petitions, documentation, mediation, settlement discussions and court representation. Clients are guided through the legal procedure and important requirements at each stage.

Property Law

Legal assistance for property transactions and disputes including sale deeds, agreements, ownership issues, partition, possession and related documentation. Documents and legal positions can be reviewed before taking important property-related decisions.

Corporate Law

Legal support for businesses and corporate requirements including agreements, contracts, commercial documentation, legal notices and compliance-related matters. The aim is to provide clear legal guidance for day-to-day business and dispute-related requirements.

Bail Matters

Legal assistance in regular bail and anticipatory bail matters, including preparation of necessary documents, understanding the applicable procedure and representation before the appropriate court in urgent or ongoing criminal proceedings.

Cheque Bounce

Legal assistance in cheque dishonour matters, including legal notices, proceedings under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, recovery-related steps and representation in appropriate proceedings.
